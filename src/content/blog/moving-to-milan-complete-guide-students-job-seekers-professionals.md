This guide covers the **first administrative steps when moving to Milan** for **students, workers, and EU citizens**. It is based on official sources from the **Comune di Milano**, **Polizia di Stato**, **Poste Italiane**, **Universitaly**, **YesMilano**, and **Agenzia delle Entrate**. Consular rules vary by nationality: always confirm with your **Italian embassy or consulate** and the [Il Visto per l'Italia](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/) wizard before you book travel.

## What this guide covers

Moving to Milan is not one form. It is a **sequence** that depends on your nationality and reason for stay:

| Profile | Entry document | First step in Italy (typical) | Residenza in Milan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Non-EU student (90+ days)** | National **type D study visa** | **Permesso di soggiorno** within **8 working days** | After valid permesso (or renewal receipt) |
| **Non-EU worker (subordinate/autonomous/family)** | Work/family visa + nulla osta route | **Sportello Unico Immigrazione** (Prefettura) then permesso | When eligible per Comune rules |
| **EU / EEA / Swiss citizen (90+ days)** | ID/passport, no Italian visa | **Codice Fiscale** + **iscrizione anagrafica** at Comune | Direct registration, no Questura permesso |
| **Short stay (≤90 days, non-EU)** | Schengen visa or visa-free if applicable | **Dichiarazione di presenza** if required, **not** a permesso | Usually not applicable |

Procedures differ by comune. This guide is **Milan-first**.

## What moving admin is and is not

**It is:**

- a fixed order: **legal entry → stay title → tax ID → daily-life setup → address registration**
- heavily **consulate-specific** before you travel
- **time-sensitive** after arrival (especially the permesso deadline)

**It is not:**

- completed by university admission alone (students still need visa + permesso + enrolment steps)
- interchangeable with a tourist Schengen stay if you plan to live and study/work long-term
- the same for EU and non-EU citizens

## Before you move: confirm visa need and pathway

### Step 0: Do you need a visa?

Use the official MAECI wizard: [vistoperitalia.esteri.it](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/). It asks nationality, country of residence, purpose, and length of stay.

YesMilano confirms: most **non-EU students staying more than 90 days** need an **entry visa** applied for at the **Italian embassy/consulate in your country of legal residence**, not in Milan ([YesMilano, student visa](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/student-visa)).

You generally cannot apply at a consulate outside your assigned district.

### Non-EU students: before departure

Official sequence from [Universitaly](https://www.universitaly.it/first-steps) and [YesMilano](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/student-visa):

1. **Choose a programme** at an Italian institution (follow their international admissions deadlines).
2. **Complete Universitaly pre-enrollment** when required: the institution validates your application online.
3. **Obtain the consular study visa** (typically type D) with documents such as passport, photos, proof of accommodation, financial means, health coverage, and enrollment/pre-enrollment proof. Visa fee: **€50** ([YesMilano: student visa](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/student-visa)).
4. Book the consular appointment **early**: YesMilano recommends allowing **~8-10 weeks** before departure; appointments may not be available for 1-2 months.

Apply **no more than 90 days before** your study term starts ([YesMilano](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/student-visa)).

**Codice Fiscale before arrival:** YesMilano notes you may request it from your **Italian consulate**, or obtain it in Milan after arrival ([YesMilano, first steps](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)). Most non-EU students receive it through the **permesso process** at the Questura ([Agenzia delle Entrate](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/codice-fiscale-e-tessera-sanitaria/che-cos-cittadini)).

### Non-EU workers and family reunification: before departure

Work and family flows usually involve the **Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione** at the **Prefettura** (nulla osta / authorization) **before** entry. The permesso kit at Poste is **not** the first step for all work types, subordinate work, autonomous work, and family reunification often start at the **SUI** ([Poste Italiane, permessi di soggiorno](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)).

Confirm your exact route with the Prefettura and your employer or consulate. Do not assume a generic "job seeker" visa exists without checking [vistoperitalia.esteri.it](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/) and consular guidance.

### EU / EEA / Swiss citizens

No Italian **permesso di soggiorno** is required. For stays **over three months**, **iscrizione anagrafica** (residence registration) at the Comune is sufficient, the same as for Italian citizens ([Poste Italiane, cittadini UE](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)).

Bring a valid **identity document**, proof of **economic resources** and **health coverage** as required for your status ([Comune di Milano, EU non-worker residency](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00491/Residenza-per-stranieri-non-lavoratori-documenti)).

## First 8 working days in Milan (non-EU long-stay)

This is the most critical deadline for most non-EU newcomers.

**Rule:** You must request a **permesso di soggiorno** within **8 working days** of entering Italy ([Polizia di Stato](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/225), [Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno), [Universitaly](https://www.universitaly.it/first-steps)).

### Students: typical Poste procedure

Study permits for **university students** use kit codice **31** (**STUDENTE**, art. 39 bis TUI) and are submitted at **Poste Italiane Sportello Amico** offices, per the official motive table ([Poste Italiane, codice 31, table updated May 2024](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)). Do not use codice **24** (motivi di studio, art. 39 TUI) or **32**/**33** (alunno/tirocinante) unless your visa category matches those rows.

**Kit:** the submission package is the **yellow-striped MOD. 209 kit** (Modulo 1 + Modulo 2, instructions, bollettino, envelope). Pick it up **free at any post office** with your passport. It is not a generic downloadable form ([Polizia di Stato, art. 217](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/217), [Portale Immigrazione: procedura](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)). Useful references before you go:

| Resource | Link |
|---|---|
| Field layout (reference PDF: do not print and submit) | [MOD_209_Multilingue.pdf](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/media/documentazione/MOD_209_Multilingue.pdf) |
| Compilation rules (field **d.** = codice motivo) | [Portale Immigrazione, kit instructions](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html) |
| Documents for **permesso per STUDIO** | [Questura, kit document list (PDF)](https://questure.poliziadistato.it/statics/29/documenti-da-inserire-nel-kit-postale-per-le-principali-tipologie-di-permesso-di-soggiorno.pdf?lang=it) |
| Sportello Amico post office near you | [Poste, find enabled office](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno) (link to office search on page) |
| Track application status (Poste phase) | [Portale Immigrazione, area riservata](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/) (codes on ricevuta) |
| Helpline (Mon-Sat 8:00-20:00) | **800.200.309** ([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)) |

**Study codice quick reference** ([Poste: motive table](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)):

| Codice | Label | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **24** | Motivi di studio (art. 39 TUI) | Older art. 39 study routes: verify against visa |
| **31** | **STUDENTE (art. 39 bis TUI)** | **Most university students** |
| **32** | Alunno (art. 39 bis TUI) | School pupils |
| **33** | Tirocinante (art. 39 bis TUI) | Internships/traineeships |

**Documents to include in the kit** (permesso per STUDIO, [Questura PDF](https://questure.poliziadistato.it/statics/29/documenti-da-inserire-nel-kit-postale-per-le-principali-tipologie-di-permesso-di-soggiorno.pdf?lang=it)):

- Dichiarazione di ospitalità + lease, or **certificato di residenza** on renewal
- Passport copy (all pages with bio data, visas, and entry stamps)
- Copy of expiring permesso (renewals only)
- **Enrollment / exams self-certification** (autocertificazione iscrizione ed esami)
- Health coverage: insurance policy **or** SSN enrollment proof (bollettino)
- Proof of **financial means**
- Marca da bollo **€16** + bollettino postale **€70.46** (study, up to 1 year, verify amount on kit)

Free kit help: authorized **Patronati** and Comuni can compile the form digitally and hand you a printout in the official envelope ([Portale Immigrazione, Patronato search](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)).

1. Pick up the **yellow-striped MOD. 209 kit** at a post office with your passport (one kit per person), or get help from an authorized **Comune/Patronato** ([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)).
2. Complete **Modulo 1** (field **d.** = **31** for most uni students) and **Modulo 2** if you have income, **sign** or the kit is rejected.
3. Submit at a **Sportello Amico** post office in an **open envelope** with A4 copies and documents above ([Portale Immigrazione, procedura](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)). Optional: [book at Poste](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno) or via the Poste Italiane app to save counter time.
4. Pay fees at the counter (see below).
5. Keep the **ricevuta** and **Questura appointment letter**, if no slot was available, a **raccomandata** follows with the date ([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)).
6. Attend **Questura di Milano** on the scheduled date with **4 identical photos** (35×45 mm, white background) and one bollettino receipt stub ([Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html), [Questura di Milano](https://questure.poliziadistato.it/it/Milano/articolo/5730e6c56f939266018879)).

Your university should provide forms and local instructions ([YesMilano, residence permit](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)).

For the full Poste procedure, kit codes, fees, and travel rules, see our [permesso di soggiorno guide](/blog/permesso-di-soggiorno-milan-newcomers).

### Workers: kit codice 16 vs 17 (do not mix up)

On **Modulo 1**, field **d.** must match your visa category ([Poste Italiane, codice table](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno), [Portale Immigrazione, kit](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)):

| Codice | Motivo | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **16** | Lavoro subordinato | Standard employment contract |
| **17** | Lavoro casi particolari art. 27 TUI | Blue Card, ICT, researchers, and other art. 27 categories |

Wrong codice = rejected or delayed application. If your route started at the **Sportello Unico Immigrazione** (nulla osta), use the kit the Prefettura gives you. Do not grab a random yellow kit at Poste.

**Processing time:** Polizia di Stato cites an **average of ~60 days** from application to electronic permesso card issuance ([Polizia di Stato](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/225)).

### Traveling while your permesso is processing

The Poste **ricevuta** is not a travel document, but Polizia di Stato allows **temporary departure and re-entry** in defined cases ([requirements for leaving Italy temporarily](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/requirements-for-immigrants-leaving-italy-temporarily)):

- **Renewals:** ricevuta + expired permesso + passport
- **First applications** (work/family routes listed on that page): ricevuta + passport + entry visa, re-entry through an **Italian external border** only; **no transit through other Schengen countries**

Confirm your case on the official page before booking flights.

### Permesso fees (indicative)

At Poste for electronic permesso production ([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Marca da bollo | €16 |
| Post office operator fee | €30.00 |
| Electronic permesso production | €30.46 |
| State contribution (study: typically 3 months-1 year) | **€40.00** ([Polizia di Stato](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/225)) |

Contributions vary by permesso duration/type: verify on [Polizia di Stato, costi](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/217) and your kit instructions.

## First 30 days: Codice Fiscale, healthcare, transport, housing

Once your permesso application is submitted, parallel setup:

### Codice Fiscale

- **Most non-EU arrivals:** assigned during **permesso processing** at the Questura ([Agenzia delle Entrate](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/codice-fiscale-e-tessera-sanitaria/che-cos-cittadini)).
- **If you still need one:** request at any **Agenzia delle Entrate** office with form **AA4/8** and valid ID, **in-person appointment required** for first issuance to foreign nationals ([Agenzia delle Entrate](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/codice-fiscale-e-tessera-sanitaria/che-cos-cittadini)).
- **Before arrival option:** Italian consulate or in Milan ([YesMilano, first steps](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)).

You need it for contracts, enrolment, and most admin.

### Healthcare

- **EU students:** may use **EHIC** in Milan; S1 form if applicable ([YesMilano, first steps](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)).
- **Non-EU full-course students:** can enroll in the **Servizio Sanitario Nazionale** at an affordable yearly rate, or maintain qualifying private insurance as required for visa/residency ([YesMilano, healthcare](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)).

For residency applications, Comune di Milano accepts **SSN enrollment** as valid health coverage for foreign students ([Comune di Milano, student residency documents](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00431/Residenza-per-studenti-stranieri-documenti)).

### Transport (Milan)

YesMilano publishes current **ATM** fares for the urban M1 zone ([YesMilano, first steps](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)):

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Single (M1-M3) | €2.20 |
| Under-27 monthly pass | €22/month |
| Regular monthly pass | €39/month |

Buy at metro stations, newsstands, or the **ATM Milano** app.

### Groceries and street markets

Once you know your **municipio**, weekly open-air markets (*mercati rionali*) are a cheap way to buy produce and household goods. The Comune publishes official locations and hours in open data (DS291). Our [**weekly markets guide**](/living-in-milan/markets) filters them **by weekday and municipio** (defaults to today). For fixed indoor halls, see [**covered markets on Milan maps**](/living-in-milan/maps#covered-markets). Official reference: [Fare Impresa Milano: mercati settimanali scoperti](https://fareimpresa.comune.milano.it/mercati-settimanali-scoperti).

More everyday maps (fountains, pharmacies, recycling): [**Living in Milan, maps**](/living-in-milan/maps).

### Housing

Secure a contract or lawful accommodation proof early, you need it for **visa**, **permesso**, and **residenza**. For rental contracts, Comune di Milano requires a **copy of the lease** (and registration receipt if applicable) ([Comune di Milano, foreign tenant housing docs](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00329/Residenza-stranieri-in-affitto-documenti-immobile)).

Keep **names, dates, and addresses identical** across passport, visa, permesso kit, and lease.

## Registering residence (residenza) in Milan

**First registration for foreigners arriving from abroad** is done **online** through Milan's dedicated service, not by walking into Anagrafe without an appointment ([Comune di Milano, KA-00489](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00489/Residenza-per-stranieri-dallestero-procedura), [Comune di Milano, residenza stranieri dall'estero](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/anagrafe/richiesta-di-residenza-per-persone-straniere-provenienti-dall-estero)). Download the required forms from the service page, upload documents, and submit within **20 days** of your transfer.

**Non-EU students** can register only with a **valid permesso di soggiorno** or documented **renewal in progress** ([Comune di Milano, student residency](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00431/Residenza-per-studenti-stranieri-documenti)).

Required documents include: valid ID, **Codice Fiscale**, proof of **school/training enrollment**, **economic resources** self-declaration (EURO1 reference), **health coverage**, and **housing documentation** ([Comune di Milano, KA-00431](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00431/Residenza-per-studenti-stranieri-documenti)).

**EU citizens** register directly with ID, resources proof, and health insurance ([Comune di Milano, KA-00491](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00491/Residenza-per-stranieri-non-lavoratori-documenti)).

**EU exchange students (4-12 months)** may use **temporary registration** instead of full residenza ([YesMilano, first steps](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)).

After residenza is accepted:

- Request the **[CIE (electronic ID)](/blog/cie-digital-id-for-expats-in-italy)**: appointment-only at Anagrafe
- File **[TARI occupancy declaration](/blog/tari-waste-tax-milan-newcomers)** within 90 days of move-in ([Comune di Milano, KA-00489](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00489/Residenza-per-stranieri-dallestero-procedura))

## Student-only: finalise university enrolment

Universitaly's last step: after visa and permesso steps, **complete enrolment at your institution** using their local procedure ([Universitaly, finalise enrolment](https://www.universitaly.it/first-steps)).

YesMilano offers orientation: **welcomestudents@yesmilano.it** and one-to-one online appointments ([YesMilano, first steps](https://studyandwork.yesmilano.it/en/study/how-to/first-steps)).

## Most common mistakes

1. **Missing the 8 working-day permesso deadline** after arrival
2. **Confusing university admission with a completed visa**, you need consular issuance
3. **Using the wrong booking channel** (e.g. national portals that do not apply to Milan-specific services)
4. **Inconsistent addresses** across visa, permesso kit, and rental contract
5. **Wrong permesso kit codice** (e.g. 24 instead of 31, or 16 instead of 17 for workers)
6. **Registering residenza before permesso** (non-EU students)
7. **Relying on random expat forums** instead of Comune, Questura, and consular pages, ikr, including this one if we ever skip the official links

## Quick checklist before and after arrival

### Before departure (non-EU student template)

- ✅ Confirmed visa need on [vistoperitalia.esteri.it](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/)
- ✅ Universitaly pre-enrollment completed and validated by your institution
- ✅ Consular visa appointment booked (~8-10 weeks lead time)
- ✅ Passport valid 3+ months beyond visa end date
- ✅ Proof of accommodation, funds, health coverage, and enrollment letter ready
- ✅ Codice Fiscale plan (consulate or after arrival)

### First 8 working days in Milan (non-EU long-stay)

- ✅ Yellow **MOD. 209 kit** picked up (or kit from **SUI/Prefettura** if your work/family route started there)
- ✅ Modulo 1 field **d.** matches your visa (see [Poste codice table](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno), e.g. **31** uni students, **16**/**17** workers)
- ✅ Motive-specific documents complete: [Questura kit PDF by type](https://questure.poliziadistato.it/statics/29/documenti-da-inserire-nel-kit-postale-per-le-principali-tipologie-di-permesso-di-soggiorno.pdf?lang=it) (students: **permesso per STUDIO** section)
- ✅ Application submitted at **Poste Sportello Amico** with all copies and payments
- ✅ Receipt and Questura appointment letter stored safely
- ✅ Questura appointment attended with 4 photos (35×45 mm)

**Students add:** enrollment/exams self-certification, health coverage proof, financial means, per STUDIO section above.

**Workers add:** confirm SUI/nulla osta route if applicable. Use Prefettura kit when required. Do not grab a random Poste kit.

### First 30-90 days

- ✅ Codice Fiscale obtained (Questura or Agenzia delle Entrate AA4/8)
- ✅ Healthcare path active (SSN or qualifying insurance)
- ✅ ATM pass or contactless payment set up
- ✅ Housing contract aligned with admin documents
- ✅ University enrolment finalised *(students only)*
- ✅ **Residenza** applied online when permesso (or renewal) allows ([Comune, stranieri dall'estero](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/anagrafe/richiesta-di-residenza-per-persone-straniere-provenienti-dall-estero))
- ✅ **TARI** occupancy declared within 90 days ([TARI guide](/blog/tari-waste-tax-milan-newcomers))
- ✅ Permesso renewal planned **60+ days before expiry** ([Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html))
