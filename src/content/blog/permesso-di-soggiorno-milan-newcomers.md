This guide covers the **permesso di soggiorno** (residence permit) for **non-EU citizens in Milan**. It is based on official sources from **Polizia di Stato**, **Poste Italiane**, **Portale Immigrazione**, and **Comune di Milano**. EU/EEA/Swiss citizens do not need a Questura permesso, they register residency at the Comune instead.

## What the permesso is

The permesso di soggiorno is Italy's **stay title** for non-EU foreigners living in Italy beyond short tourist stays. The electronic version is a smart card issued by the **Questura** after identity checks (including fingerprints).

It is separate from:

- your **passport** and entry **visa**
- **residenza anagrafica** (address registration at the Comune)
- the **CIE** (Italian ID card for residents)

## Who must request it

| Profile | Permesso required? | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-EU, stay >90 days** | Yes | Poste (most types) or Questura/SUI (see below) |
| **Non-EU, tourist/business ≤90 days** | No, use **dichiarazione di presenza** if applicable | N/A |
| **EU / EEA / Swiss** | No Questura permesso | [Comune residenza registration](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/anagrafe/richiesta-di-residenza-per-persone-straniere-provenienti-dall-estero) |

**Deadline:** request within **8 working days** of entering Italy ([Polizia di Stato, art. 225](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/225), [Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)).

## What it is and is not

**It is:**

- your legal right to remain in Italy for a specific **motivo di soggiorno** (study, work, family, etc.)
- required before most **residenza** applications for non-EU newcomers
- often applied for at **Poste Sportello Amico**, then finalized at the **Questura**

**It is not:**

- a travel document: use your **passport** for leaving and re-entering Italy
- interchangeable with a Schengen tourist stay
- the same kit for every motive. Check the **codice** on your yellow-striped kit matches your visa reason

## Poste vs Questura vs Sportello Unico (SUI)

| Route | When |
|---|---|
| **Poste Sportello Amico** | Most common motives (students, workers, family, etc.), see codice table below |
| **Questura directly** | Motives **not** on the Poste list ([Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)) |
| **Sportello Unico Immigrazione (Prefettura)** | **First** work/family permits with **nulla osta**, kit is handed to you at the SUI, then submitted at Poste ([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)) |

Do not pick up a random yellow kit if your route starts at the **Prefettura**, the SUI provides the correct pre-filled package.

## Kit codice: do not mix up 16 and 17

When you complete **Modulo 1**, field **d.** asks for the **codice motivo** from the kit tables ([Portale Immigrazione, kit instructions](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)). Two work codes are often confused:

| Codice | Motivo | Typical visa holder |
|---|---|---|
| **16** | **Lavoro subordinato** (standard employed work) | Regular employment contract |
| **17** | **Lavoro casi particolari art. 27 TUI** | Blue Card, ICT, researchers, and other special work categories listed in art. 27 |

Using the **wrong codice** can delay or reject your application. Match the code to your **visa sticker** and nulla osta category. When in doubt, ask your employer, university, or a **Patronato** listed on [Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html) before submitting.

**Students** typically use codice **31** (STUDENTE, art. 39 bis). Related study codes from the [Poste motive table](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno):

| Codice | Label | When |
|---|---|---|
| **24** | Motivi di studio (art. 39 TUI) | Art. 39 routes: verify against visa |
| **31** | **STUDENTE (art. 39 bis TUI)** | **Most university students** |
| **32** | Alunno (art. 39 bis TUI) | School pupils |
| **33** | Tirocinante (art. 39 bis TUI) | Internships |

## Step-by-step: Poste route (most newcomers)

1. **Get the yellow-striped kit** at a post office (one kit per person, with passport or existing permesso), or use free help from an authorized **Comune/Patronato**.
2. Complete **Modulo 1** (and **Modulo 2** if you have income). Sign in the designated box: unsigned kits are rejected ([Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)).
3. Gather copies: passport pages (bio + visa stamps), supporting docs for your motive, payment receipts.
4. Submit at a **Sportello Amico** post office in an **open envelope** ([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno)).
5. Pay at the counter (marca da bollo, operator fee, production fee, state contribution).
6. Keep the **ricevuta** and **Questura appointment letter** (or await raccomandata if no slot was available).
7. Attend **Questura** on the scheduled date with **4 identical photos** (35×45 mm, white background) and payment receipt stub ([Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)).

**Processing:** Polizia di Stato cites an **average of ~60 days** from application to electronic card issuance ([Polizia di Stato, art. 225](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/225)).

## Documents checklist (Poste submission)

Always bring:

- Valid **passport** (or equivalent travel document)
- **Modulo 1** (+ **Modulo 2** if applicable), signed
- **A4 photocopies** of passport bio page and visa/entry stamps
- **Marca da bollo** (€16) affixed to the application
- **Bollettino postale** payment receipt(s) for production + contribution
- Motive-specific documents per [Portale Immigrazione tables](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)

For **renewals**, also include a photocopy of your **expiring permesso**.

## Fees (indicative)

At Poste for electronic permesso production ([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno), [Polizia di Stato, art. 217](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/217)):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Marca da bollo | €16 |
| Post office operator fee | €30.00 |
| Electronic permesso production | €30.46 |
| State contribution (3 months-1 year) | **€40.00** |
| State contribution (1-2 years) | **€50.00** |
| State contribution (long-term / special categories) | **€100.00** |

Contributions vary by duration and type. Minors under 18 and certain protection statuses are exempt ([Polizia di Stato, art. 217](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/217)).

## After you apply: ricevuta, travel, and renewal

### Ricevuta (application receipt)

The Poste **ricevuta** proves you submitted your application. Attach it to:

- your **passport** (first applications), or
- your **expiring permesso** (renewals)

([Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno))

It allows you to **circulate inside Italy** while waiting. It is **not** a passport substitute for international travel.

### Traveling with the ricevuta

Polizia di Stato rules for temporary departure ([requirements for leaving Italy temporarily](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/requirements-for-immigrants-leaving-italy-temporarily)):

**Renewals (and similar):** you may leave and re-enter Italy with:

- the Poste **ricevuta**
- your **expired permesso**
- your **passport**

**First applications** (work subordinate, autonomous, or family reunification): facilitated re-entry is allowed only if you:

- enter and exit through an **Italian external border** (not transit through other Schengen countries)
- carry passport, entry visa, and Poste ricevuta

Rules differ by case: confirm against the official page before booking travel.

### Renewal timing

Renew at least **60 days before expiry** via Poste when your motive is on the Poste list ([Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/ITA/nuovaProcedura.html)). Track status on [Portale Immigrazione](https://www.portaleimmigrazione.it/) (codes on your ricevuta) and, after Questura fingerprinting, on [Polizia di Stato](https://www.poliziadistato.it/).

### Questura appointments

Book or manage appointments via [PrenotaFacile](https://prenotafacile.poliziadistato.it/) when needed. Milan's Questura is **Questura di Milano**: verify address on the convocation letter.

## What comes next

Once your permesso (or renewal ricevuta) is in place:

1. **[Register residenza online](/blog/moving-to-milan-complete-guide-students-job-seekers-professionals#registering-residence-residenza-in-milan)**: Milan's first registration for foreigners from abroad is via the dedicated online service, not a walk-in Anagrafe appointment ([Comune di Milano, KA-00489](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00489/Residenza-per-stranieri-dallestero-procedura)).
2. **[Request the CIE](/blog/cie-digital-id-for-expats-in-italy)** after residenza is registered.
3. **[Declare TARI occupancy](/blog/tari-waste-tax-milan-newcomers)** within 90 days of moving in ([Comune di Milano, KA-00489](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00489/Residenza-per-stranieri-dallestero-procedura)).

See also our [moving to Milan overview](/blog/moving-to-milan-complete-guide-students-job-seekers-professionals) for visa, healthcare, and housing sequence.

## Quick checklist before Poste

- ✅ Within **8 working days** of entry
- ✅ Yellow kit with correct **codice motivo** (**31** students; 16 vs 17 workers)
- ✅ Modulo 1 signed; Modulo 2 if you have income
- ✅ Passport + A4 copies (bio + visa pages)
- ✅ Motive-specific supporting documents
- ✅ Marca da bollo €16 + bollettino payments ready
- ✅ Sportello Amico post office located ([Poste office search](https://www.poste.it/guida-rilascio-e-rinnovo-permesso-di-soggiorno))
- ✅ Questura appointment letter stored safely
- ✅ Renewal planned **60+ days before expiry**
