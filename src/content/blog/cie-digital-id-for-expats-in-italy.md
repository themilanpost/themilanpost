This guide covers the **Carta d'Identità Elettronica (CIE)** for **foreign residents in Milan**. It is based on official sources from the **Comune di Milano** and the **Ministero dell'Interno**. Procedures and fees can differ in other municipalities. Always confirm on your comune's website before booking.

## What the CIE is

The CIE is Italy's electronic identity card. It is both:

- a **physical ID document** (polycarbonate card with chip, photo, and fingerprints when applicable), and
- a **digital identity** for accessing many Italian public services online via the [CieID app](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/info-utili/cie-id/) and **Entra con CIE**.

In Milan, the CIE has replaced the old paper carta d'identità for new requests and renewals.

## Who can request it in Milan

You must apply **in person** at an Anagrafe office. General prerequisites:

| Requirement | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Residenza anagrafica** registered in Italy | You apply at your comune of residence (or domicilio, in limited cases). |
| **Valid stay title** (non-EU) | Extra-UE citizens need a valid **permesso di soggiorno** or **carta di soggiorno**, or proof of renewal (see below). |
| **Codice fiscale** | Tessera sanitaria or codice fiscale speeds up registration. |

**EU citizens** with registered residency follow the standard first-request document list (no permesso di soggiorno required for the CIE application itself).

**Extra-UE citizens** must also present permesso di soggiorno or carta di soggiorno. If the permit is expired, Milan accepts the **renewal receipt** or a **Questura appointment confirmation** via the [PrenotaFacile](https://prenotafacile.poliziadistato.it/) app, per [Comune di Milano FAQ (extra-UE)](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00393/Prima-CIE-per-cittadini-extra-UE-documenti-necessari).

## What the CIE is and is not, for expats

**Inside Italy:** the CIE is a standard identity document for banks, contracts, public offices, and everyday admin.

**For travel abroad (critical):**

- For **Italian citizens**, a CIE valid for espatrio can be used in the EU and in Iceland, Norway, Switzerland, and Liechtenstein ([Comune di Milano](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00472/Validita-della-CIE-allestero)).
- For **foreign residents**, the identity card issued in Italy is **not valid for espatrio**. Use your **passport** (or other travel document from your country of citizenship) to leave Italy. This is stated explicitly for foreign residents by the [Prefettura di Roma, Ministero dell'Interno](https://prefettura.interno.gov.it/it/prefetture/roma/stranieri-e-anagrafe) and confirmed for foreign minors by [Comune di Milano](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00469/CIE-ed-espatrio-per-minori-stranieri).

The CIE **does not replace** your permesso di soggiorno. You must keep your stay title valid independently.

## Step 1: Book an appointment (Milan)

CIE requests in Milan are **appointment-only**.

- **Use:** [Comune di Milano: Carta d'identità service page](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/anagrafe/carta-d-identita) (booking links at top of page).
- **Do not use** the national portal `agendacie.interno.gov.it` for Milan, the comune uses its **own booking system** ([Comune di Milano FAQ](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-03767/Informazione-prenotazione-appuntamento-portale-nazionale)).
- **SPID is not required** for booking ([Comune di Milano FAQ](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00578/Prenotazione-appuntamento-per-rilascio-CIE)). You can also use the simplified login at [servizicrm.comune.milano.it/appuntamenti/login](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/appuntamenti/login) (name, surname, email).

### FAQ: codice fiscale not recognized when booking

If the booking portal rejects your **codice fiscale** or shows an error after login:

1. Clear **cache and cookies** and try an updated browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) ([Comune di Milano, KA-03729](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-03729/Messaggio-errore-dopo-login-cosa-fare))
2. Switch browser or try desktop vs mobile
3. Confirm your CF matches your **tessera sanitaria** exactly (same spelling as passport)
4. If you received CF very recently (e.g. at Questura), allow time for registry sync, then retry
5. Still blocked? Submit the online help form under **"Hai ancora bisogno d'aiuto?"** on the [Comune support portal](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto), noting date, browser, and a screenshot

CIE booking itself does **not** require SPID, only the simplified form or service-page flow ([Comune di Milano, KA-00578](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00578/Prenotazione-appuntamento-per-rilascio-CIE)).

On appointment day, complete check-in using the link in your confirmation email, or show the QR code, codice fiscale, or existing CIE at the desk ([Prenota appuntamento in Comune](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/prenota-il-tuo-appuntamento-in-comune)).

## Step 2: Documents to bring

### First request (general)

Per [Comune di Milano](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00552/Prima-richiesta-CIE-documenti-necessari):

- Tessera sanitaria **or** codice fiscale
- One **paper** passport photo meeting ICAO standards (see below)
- Another valid ID document (passport, driving licence, etc.) **or** two adult witnesses with valid ID

### First request: extra-UE citizens

Add to the list above ([Comune di Milano, extra-UE](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00393/Prima-CIE-per-cittadini-extra-UE-documenti-necessari)):

- Permesso di soggiorno or carta di soggiorno (valid, or renewal receipt / PrenotaFacile booking if expired)

### Renewal

Per [Comune di Milano: general renewal](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/anagrafe/carta-d-identita) and [extra-UE renewal FAQ](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00564/Rinnovo-CIE-per-cittadini-extra-UE-documenti-richiesti):

- Tessera sanitaria or codice fiscale
- One paper passport photo
- Expired or damaged CIE (or theft/loss report to police, if applicable)
- Permesso di soggiorno (extra-UE, same rules as first request)

You can renew up to **180 days before expiry** ([Ministero dell'Interno](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/richiedi/rilascio-e-rinnovo-in-italia/)).

### Photo requirements (Milan)

Milan accepts **paper photos only**: not USB/digital uploads ([Comune di Milano](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00414/Formato-foto-CIE-solo-fototessere-cartacee)).

- **Size:** 35 × 45 mm, ICAO/passport standard ([Comune di Milano, photo FAQ](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00308/Formato-foto-CIE-requisiti-dimensioni))
- Recent (within 6 months), neutral expression, plain light background, face uncovered
- Some Anagrafe offices have validated photo booths (see the [service page](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/anagrafe/carta-d-identita))

Technical photo rules from the Ministry: [Modalità di acquisizione foto](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/richiedi/modalita-di-acquisizione-foto/).

## Step 3: Appointment day, payment, and delivery

### Payment

**€22.20** at the desk on appointment day, cash or card ([Comune di Milano, cost FAQ](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00305/Costo-della-CIE-e-modalita-di-pagamento)).

National base fee is €16.79 plus local fees ([Ministero dell'Interno](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/richiedi/rilascio-e-rinnovo-in-italia/)); Milan's total is €22.20.

### You do not receive the card immediately

The CIE is printed by the Istituto Poligrafico e Zecca dello Stato and **shipped within 6 working days** ([Comune di Milano service page](https://www.comune.milano.it/servizi/anagrafe/carta-d-identita), [Ministero dell'Interno](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/richiedi/rilascio-e-rinnovo-in-italia/)).

- Delivery is by **registered mail (raccomandata)** to an address you provide, home or another domicilio
- You **cannot collect it at the Anagrafe desk** ([Comune di Milano FAQ](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00304/Validita-ricevuta-CIE-solo-per-lItalia))
- Provide a complete address (street, number, CAP, city, floor/door if applicable) and ensure your name is on the mailbox/citofono

### Receipt after the appointment

At the end of the appointment you receive a **ricevuta** (receipt with photo and data). It is valid **only for identification inside Italy**, **not for travel abroad** ([Comune di Milano](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00304/Validita-ricevuta-CIE-solo-per-lItalia)).

### PIN and PUK

The CIE PIN is delivered in **two parts**: first part at the identification appointment, second part when you receive the card ([Agenzia delle Entrate, access guide PDF](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/0/FolderAccessoServizi_dicembre2023.pdf/a8a3b77b-ca1a-9561-d1ab-ddb8a66b4100)). Store both securely: they are required for digital use.

## Validity

Per [Comune di Milano](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-00517/Durata-di-validita-della-CIE):

| Age group | Validity |
|---|---|
| Under 3 | 3 years |
| 3-17 | 5 years |
| Adults | ~10 years (9 years plus days until birthday) |
| Temporary fingerprint exemption | 1 year |

## Using the CIE online (after you receive it)

Once you have the physical card and full PIN:

1. Install **[CieID](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/info-utili/cie-id/)** (Android 6+ or iOS 13+)
2. Register the card in the app (8-digit PIN + NFC tap on supported phones)
3. On supported portals, choose **Entra con CIE**, [tutorial](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/info-utili/entra-con-cie/)

**Examples of services that support CIE login:**

- [INPS](https://www.inps.it/it/it/assistenza/cie---carta-d-identit--elettronica.html)
- [Agenzia delle Entrate: area riservata](https://assistenza.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/accedere-all-area-riservata) (CIE section)
- [Fascicolo Sanitario Elettronico: access with CIE](https://support.fascicolo-sanitario.it/guida/accedi-al-tuo-fse/accedi-con-cie)
- ANPR-related municipal certificate services ([innovazione.gov.it overview](https://innovazione.gov.it/notizie/articoli/dal-15-novembre-certificati-anagrafici-online-e-gratuiti-per-i-cittadini/))

Coverage is **not universal** on every local or private portal. Many services also accept **SPID**: keeping both is practical.

### CIE vs SPID and the Fascicolo del Cittadino

Both are valid **certified digital identities** for Milan's online services, but they work differently:

| | **SPID** | **CIE** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Separate identity from a provider (PosteID, Aruba, etc.) | Tied to your physical CIE card + PIN |
| **Fascicolo del Cittadino** | ✅ Accepted | ✅ Accepted |
| **CIE appointment booking** | Not required | Not required (simplified login works) |
| **Before you have a CIE** | Often the fastest first option | Requires card + PIN + NFC/CieID app |

The **Fascicolo del Cittadino** (Milan's citizen portal for TARI, certificates, and municipal services) accepts **only SPID or CIE**, no Comune username/password ([Comune di Milano, KA-03726](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-03726/Credenziali-accesso-Fascicolo-del-Cittadino), [KA-03731](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-03731/Accesso-al-Fascicolo-del-Cittadino-senza-SPID-o-CIE)).

**Practical order for newcomers:**

1. Use **SPID** (or consulate-issued CF) to access [Fascicolo del Cittadino](https://fascicolo.comune.milano.it/) for TARI declarations and municipal services. See our [TARI guide](/blog/tari-waste-tax-milan-newcomers)
2. After **residenza** and CIE issuance, add **CieID** so Entra con CIE works on national portals (INPS, Agenzia delle Entrate, FSE)

If CIE login to Fascicolo fails despite valid PIN and NFC, see [Comune di Milano, CIE not recognized (KA-03728)](https://servizicrm.comune.milano.it/centro-supporto/KA-03728/CIE-non-riconosciuta-come-fare).

For other PA portals, coverage is **not universal**, keeping **both** SPID and CIE active is practical.

## Quick checklist before you go

- ✅ Residenza registered in Milan
- ✅ Valid permesso (extra-UE) or renewal proof
- ✅ Codice fiscale / tessera sanitaria
- ✅ One ICAO paper photo (35×45 mm)
- ✅ Valid passport or other ID (or two witnesses)
- ✅ Appointment booked on **comune.milano.it** (not the national Agenda CIE portal)
- ✅ €22.20 for payment at desk
- ✅ Mailbox/citofono name ready for raccomandata delivery
